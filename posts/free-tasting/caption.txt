A waived tasting fee isn't free.

Example numbers: five pours is about a third of a bottle, so at $9 of wine cost a bottle that's about $3 poured. Add 25 minutes of staff time at $22 an hour, about $9. One tasting costs about $12 before anyone buys.

Waive a $25 fee and that tasting cost $12 plus the $25 you gave up: about $37. Two $30 bottles carry about $42 of margin, which covers it, barely. One bottle doesn't.

Many wineries waive the fee with a purchase. The question is which purchase. Count the waivers, and what those guests bought.
