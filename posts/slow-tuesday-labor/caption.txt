Your labor % looks fine for the week. Tuesday is the problem.

Example numbers: Saturday does $9,000 with five people, $880 in wages, about 10%. Tuesday does $1,200 with two people, $352 in wages, 29%. The week comes out under 13%, so nobody looks.

Staff from your own history instead. If the last eight Tuesdays sold $900 to $1,500, usually $1,200, then at a 20% target and $22 an hour that day supports about 11 hours, not 16. Keep the floor you need to open safely, and move the rest to Saturday.
