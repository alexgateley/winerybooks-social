Club shipment month looks like your best month. It isn't.

Example numbers: 300 members at $180 a shipment, charged in one week, is $54,000. If the tasting room does $40,000 a month, March shows $94,000 and April shows $40,000. March wasn't great and April didn't collapse. The club landed, that's all.

Three runs a year is $162,000, about $13,500 a month if you spread it. So read them apart: the club, run against run (members charged, average charge, refunds); the tasting room, against the same month last year.

If members pay before the wine ships, ask your CPA how to book it.
